# D-6 · Relationship to OrangeCheck

Status: Open

## Context

[OrangeCheck](https://github.com/orangecheck/oc-attest-protocol) is live on mainnet. It links a Bitcoin address to a Nostr key with a BIP-322 signed message and measures unspent coins that can move at any time. Bondpost makes a forward commitment that consensus enforces, bound to the identity on chain.

## Options

1. Stay separate, as the timelocked alternative.
2. Also publish Bondpost proofs in a form OrangeCheck verifiers can read.

## Decision

Not yet made. Decide before the draft NIP.
