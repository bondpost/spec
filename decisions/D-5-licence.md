## Context

The implementation is proposed as MIT or Apache-2.0, matching the Rust Bitcoin libraries. The spec text may need a different kind of licence: specs are often released under CC-BY-4.0 or CC0 so they can be copied into a NIP or BIP freely.

## Options

- One licence for both repositories (MIT or Apache-2.0).
